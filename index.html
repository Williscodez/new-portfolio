About Me

I'm a computer engineering student who builds for the web and for the people who use it. My first big project was an E-Voting System with Pastech Solutions, a team run by my elective ICT tutor in senior high. I worked on it alongside my tutor and a few classmates, and the platform reached over 5,000 user inputs.

Since then we've built a responsive website for Prempeh College, which passed 1,000 visits in its first month, and I built NexusLearn, an e-learning platform for West African senior high students. Alongside the code, I shoot photo and video and run sound and livestreams, so I care how something looks and feels as much as whether it works.

These days I'm in my second year at GCTU, adding C++, Arduino and MATLAB to my web skills. Away from the screen you'll find me listening to music, cooking, or picking up a new hobby. Always remember: slow motion is better than no motion.
